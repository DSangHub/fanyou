Conditions, Risks & Limitations

Conditions

  • Any future funding offer requires legal and athletic-eligibility review and approval before money can be accepted.
  • Participants must complete identity and age verification. Funding involving minors requires separate legal review and any required guardian, court, and athletic approvals.
  • A signed agreement must state the funding purpose, amount, interest and fees, repayment schedule or income formula, maximum total repayment, and hardship, default, cancellation, and dispute terms.
  • The agreement must explain whether repayment is owed without a professional career. Repayment terms must be agreed before funding; changes require the agreed amendment process.
  • Any payroll repayment must comply with applicable law and use a separate voluntary authorization. It must not be a condition of employment.

Risks

  • Fans could lose some or all of their contribution. Earnings, repayment, scholarships, and a professional career are not guaranteed.
  • Injury, illness, unemployment, changes in income, or an ended sports career may delay or prevent repayment. A signed repayment promise does not eliminate loss risk.
  • Players may owe repayments even if they never turn professional, depending on the contract. Understand the full cost and hardship terms before agreeing.
  • Funding arrangements may affect athletic eligibility and create tax obligations. Each offer needs an individual review.

Limitations

  • This is a proposed program. FanYou is not currently offering investments or loans or accepting player-funding contributions.
  • No interest rate, return, repayment formula, cash-out, resale, or withdrawal right has been established. Availability will depend on approved offers and applicable law.
  • Profile verification confirms reviewed identity or affiliation; it does not establish creditworthiness, ability to repay, or investment quality.
  • Fans receive no ownership of a Player or control over their school, employer, team, or career choices.
  • FanYou points and stars are separate from financial contributions and do not represent investment ownership or a right to financial returns.

This overview is informational and is not a funding offer or loan agreement. Final terms and legally required disclosures must be provided before any future transaction.
